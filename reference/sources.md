## Source Trust Tiers

**Tier 1 — Primary** (prefer these; cite directly):
Government agencies, industry associations, major wire services (Reuters, Bloomberg via public articles)

**Tier 2 — Secondary** (use to corroborate Tier 1):
Specialized trade publications (Fibre2Fashion, TextileWorld, Just-Style)

**Tier 3 — Supplementary** (use only if Tier 1/2 unavailable):
General business news, regional outlets

**Excluded** (never cite):
- Anonymous forums, social media posts, company press releases without third-party corroboration
- Sources behind full paywalls where content cannot be verified
- AI-generated news aggregators without original reporting

---

## Deduplication Note

When the same story appears in multiple sources, cite all Tier 1 sources that carried it.
Use the most authoritative source as the primary citation; list others as "also reported by".
